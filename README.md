### 👋 Hi, I'm Frances Laureano De Leon!

I'm a **Research Fellow at Centre-UB, University of Birmingham, UK**, working at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing (NLP), social science, and behavioural research**.

🔬 **Research interests:** I'm interested in developing and applying NLP methods to better understand human behaviour, communication, and social phenomena. My broader research interests include language model understanding, multilingual NLP, and the capabilities and limitations of large language models (LLMs).

🤝 **Collaborations:** I'm keen to collaborate on interdisciplinary projects that bring together NLP, computational methods, and social and behavioural sciences, particularly those exploring how language technologies can support social science research.
